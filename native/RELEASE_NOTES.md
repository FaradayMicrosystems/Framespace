# Framespace v0.28 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it over v0.27.1. It keeps your saved rooms.

## Tools menu (stick click)
- Now: Rotate/Move, Scale, Edit, Player move, **Tape, Area, Volume, Box draw, Grid**, Reset position. Circle 3-pt, Rect 3-pt, Height, Angle 3-pt and Path are gone.
- **Tape:** a real tape measure in mid-air. The point is the yellow dot 10 cm off the controller tip (not stuck to surfaces). Trigger at one end, then the other; or hold the trigger, drag, let go. The line snaps to the nearest 45° unless you hold grip.
- **Area:** same dot; points snap onto the floor (or a surface) when close.
- **Volume:** like Area, then after you close the outline raise or lower the dot for the height and pull the trigger. The solid drops into the world with its volume.
- **Box draw:** the dot sits 50 cm off the tip and snaps onto the floor. Trigger at one corner, move to the opposite corner, squeeze grip and raise your hand for the height, let go to lock it, trigger to drop the box (size and volume shown).
- **Grid:** each press steps 10 → 5 → 1 cm → 10. Default 10 cm. (Moved here from Settings.)

## Objects menu (Y / B)
- 4 × 4, scrolls with the thumbstick up / down while you point at it.
- Rides 25 cm straight out in front of the controller that opened it, like a palette. Tap Y / B to show / hide it.
- Picking an object drops it straight in front of you: near edge 50 cm from your head (2 m for things over 1.5 m like vehicles, containers, garages), onto the floor or whatever is below.
- **Rename custom objects:** Custom... > Rename: on, pick one, type on the on-screen keyboard, OK.

## Seeing and pointing
- **Line of sight:** the pointer stops at the first thing you can see. You can't select through other objects (pinned or not) or through real walls.
- **Farizon V7E open cargo:** open rear and open sliding-door openings on both sides; you can see and reach into the cargo area from outside. Cabs on the Stockman, F3E and V7E no longer block the view above the bonnet.
