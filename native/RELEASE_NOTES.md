# Framespace v0.29 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it over v0.28. It keeps your saved rooms.

## Objects menu (Y / B)
- **Categories** in the sidebar: Pallets & boxes, Containers & trailers, Vehicles, Buildings, Kitchen, Furniture, Shelving & storage, Racks, Workshop (plus Custom...). Categories come from `#= Name` lines in objects.csv.
- **Box draw** now lives in the sidebar where Box was (toggle on / off). It's gone from the Tools menu.
- **Tap Y / B:** the menu rides 25 cm in front of that controller; only your other controller can point at it and grab.
- **Hold Y / B:** the menu stays put 45 cm out; point with the same controller, scroll with its stick, trigger-grab to pick (closes it).

## Clipboard menu (X / A)
- **Rename** (on-screen keyboard) replaces Bigger / Smaller 5%. Resizing lives in the Scale tool. Undo restores the old name.

## Two-hand resize
- **Proportions locked:** pulling your hands apart / together scales the whole object evenly. No more stretched utes.

## Grid
- Default **1 cm**. Tools > Grid steps 1 → 10 → 5 → 1 cm.
