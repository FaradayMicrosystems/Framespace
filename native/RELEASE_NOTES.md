# Framespace v0.27 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it (see [native/README.md](https://github.com/FaradayMicrosystems/Framespace/blob/main/native/README.md)). It installs over v0.26 and keeps your saved rooms.

## Changed
- **Occlusion (hidden-line wireframes).** Objects stay wireframes, but lines behind another object are no longer drawn, so you only see what's in front. Walk-in containers, the H9E box and garages occlude with their walls (you still see in through open doors); vans and utes with their side profile; detailed objects with their real parts (legs and gaps stay see-through). Passthrough is unchanged.
- **Settings (left ≡) > Occlusion: on / off** if you want see-through wireframes back. It's remembered.

(Also in this line: v0.26 stacks move together; v0.25 pinned = locked and the Tools menu picks on trigger.)
