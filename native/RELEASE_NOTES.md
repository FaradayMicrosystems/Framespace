# Framespace v0.24 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it (see [native/README.md](https://github.com/FaradayMicrosystems/Framespace/blob/main/native/README.md)). It installs over v0.23 and keeps your saved rooms.

## Controls
- **Objects menu (Y / B)** pops up 70 cm in front of the controller and stays put (no more wrist menu).
  - Tap: it's a palette. Trigger-grab as many objects as you like; tap again to close.
  - Hold and let go: picks nothing, the menu stays up. Trigger-grab one object and it closes.
- **Two-hand resize:** carry an object, grab it with your other hand (trigger), pull apart / push together to stretch it along that direction, turn your hands to turn it. Smooth, no snapping. Let go with the second hand to lock the size. One undo.
- **Spawn distance:** picked objects appear with their nearest edge at least 3 m away, still on your pointer.
- **X / A with your hand inside an object** targets that object.
