# Framespace v0.31 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it over v0.30. It keeps your saved rooms.

23 changes, all from Aurora's list. Built for planning mobile workshops shipped to sites.

## Walls
- **Wall draw** (Y / B objects menu sidebar, under Box draw): the yellow dot 50 cm off the tip snaps to the floor. **Trigger** at each corner and a wall goes up between clicks (2.4 m high, 110 mm thick, AU stud wall). **Click the first corner** to close the room. **Grip** removes the last wall. **Stick up / down** changes the height of the next walls in 10 cm steps. Walls are normal objects: Edit stretches them, they save with the room, walls meeting at corners don't count as "doesn't fit".
- **Walls category:** wall panels 1 / 2 / 3 m (2.4 m high) and a 2 m x 1.2 m partition.

## Wall mount (replaces Shelving & storage)
- **Hold a wall-mount object within about 12 cm of a wall** (Space Setup walls, the room mesh, drawn walls, wall panels, container sides) and it turns flat against it at the height you're holding it. Let go: it stays on the wall. Pull it further away: it comes off. Picking one from the menu while pointing at a wall puts it straight on the wall.
- **Drill guide label:** centre and bottom height above the floor, and the distance to the walls either side.
- **Mounted things move with what they're on:** move a drawn wall or a container and its shelves, screens and lights go too (and cartons on a shelf ride along).
- **One floating shelf** (900 x 250): the 5-30 cm stacking shelves and both spacers are gone.
- **Steel shelving 1800** (was Garage shelving): stands on the floor, backs flush onto a wall.
- **Monitors** 24 / 27 / 32 in (16:9) and 34 in ultrawide, on slim VESA mounts. The 32 in desk monitor stays in Furniture.
- **Workshop wall gear:** steel pegboard 1200 x 600, steel wall cabinet, fold-down bench 1500 (open), cantilever stock rack, air hose reel, electrical lead reel, 3-phase distribution board, welding fume extraction arm (2 m), wall exhaust fan, 7 kW split-system AC head, 4.5 kg fire extinguisher on bracket, fire blanket, workplace first aid cabinet, eye wash station, gas cylinder bracket (2 x G).
