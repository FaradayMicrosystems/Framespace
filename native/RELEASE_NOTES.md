# Framespace v0.26 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it (see [native/README.md](https://github.com/FaradayMicrosystems/Framespace/blob/main/native/README.md)). It installs over v0.25 and keeps your saved rooms.

## Changed
- **Stacks move together.** Whatever sits on an object rides with it when you slide, lift, carry or turn it: cartons on a pallet, a pallet in the H9E or a container, a dryer on a washer, a whole stack of shelves. It works all the way up the stack, and the stack stays together when you put it down. One undo puts everything back.
- Pinned objects never ride along (pinned = locked where it is).

(v0.25 also in this line: pinned = fully locked; stick-click Tools menu pops up 70 cm ahead and picks on trigger.)
