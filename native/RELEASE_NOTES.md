# Framespace v0.25 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it (see [native/README.md](https://github.com/FaradayMicrosystems/Framespace/blob/main/native/README.md)). It installs over v0.24 and keeps your saved rooms.

## Changed
- **Pinned = locked.** A pinned object can't be moved, slid, lifted, turned, resized (Scale, Edit, two-hand, Bigger/Smaller, Reset), dropped, cut, copied, duplicated or deleted, and Clear all leaves it in place. Only Unpin (X / A menu) works on it.
- **Tools menu (stick click)** works like the objects menu: one click pops it up 70 cm in front of that controller and it stays put. Point at a tool (either hand), pull the trigger: it's picked and the menu closes. Click the stick again to close it without picking.
