# Framespace v0.30 (Quest 3 APK)

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it over v0.29. It keeps your saved rooms.

Early release (2 changes) at Aurora's request.

## New: Power category (objects menu)
- **EcoFlow:** RIVER 3, RIVER 3 Plus + extra batteries 286 Wh / 572 Wh, DELTA 3 Plus + DELTA 3 extra battery (1 kWh), DELTA 3 Max Plus + its extra battery (2 kWh), DELTA Pro 3 (telescopic handle, wheels) + DELTA Pro 3 extra battery (4 kWh).
- **DJI:** Power 500, Power 1000 Mini, Power 1000 / 1000 V2, Power 2000, Power Expansion Battery 2000.
- Real dimensions from the makers' spec sheets; wireframes with display, AU outlets, USB ports, vents, handles and feet. Extra batteries have flat tops and **stack** on their power station (the stack moves together).
- **Dune 4WD 300 W solar blanket**, unfolded at 45°: 5 panels (best guess 550 mm square) with 2 cm case strips, cell grid, stand legs; about 2.83 m long.
