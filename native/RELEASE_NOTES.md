# Framespace v0.21a (Quest 3 APK)

First versioned release. It brings together all the work from the last three days (PRs #9, #10 and #11) in one build.

**Install:** download `framespace.apk` below and sideload it (see [native/README.md](https://github.com/FaradayMicrosystems/Framespace/blob/main/native/README.md)). It installs over older builds and keeps your saved rooms.

## Controls
- **Stick click:** main menu on that hand (tools and settings). Both sticks: fixed in front of you.
- **X / A:** objects menu on that hand. Trigger or grip an object with the other hand to grab it.
- **Y / B:** clipboard menu on that hand. Pointing at a shape: Cut, Copy, Delete, Duplicate, Pin, Drop, Bigger/Smaller 5%, Reset size, Undo. Pointing at empty space: Paste, Next clip, Undo.
- **Trigger on an object:** pick it up on the pointer and twist your wrist to turn it. It snaps onto surfaces it touches. A quick tap only selects.
- **Grip:** slide on the floor grid. It steps up onto container and van floors.
- **Grip + trigger:** lift it.
- **Stick while holding:** left/right turns it, forward/back pushes it away or pulls it in.

## Tools
- Measuring: Tape, Circle 3-pt, Rect 3-pt, Height, Angle 3-pt, Path, Area.
- **Accuracy test:** measure known references and get average, worst, bias and RMS error, plus a CSV export.
- **Room info:** floor area, ceiling height, volume, walls, doors and windows from Space Setup.
- **Fit check:** objects that push into walls, furniture or each other turn red.
- Scale tool: resize cubes.

## Objects
27 true-scale objects. The walk-in 20ft and 40ft containers (doors open), the V7E open cargo and the F3E trayback are hollow, so you can load pallets into them. Forklift, pallet jack, pallets, IBC and motorcycle have detailed wireframes.
