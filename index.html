Controls

  • Point + trigger (or pinch with hands): press menu buttons, grab and move shapes, place tape points.
  • Grip button on a shape also grabs it. While holding: thumbstick up/down pushes it away / pulls it closer, left/right spins it.
  • Yellow cubes on the selected shape are resize handles. Point, hold trigger, drag. Orange cubes change the radius.
  • Tape: two points on any real or virtual surface. Shows length plus vertical and horizontal parts. Points snap to corners and earlier points.
  • Circle 3-pt: three points on a round edge (pipe, table, bucket) gives diameter, radius and circumference.
  • Copy / Cut / Paste: Paste drops the shown clipboard item where you point. Next clip cycles the last 5 copies.
  • Shapes snap to the floor and to tables within 3 cm. Drop lowers the selected shape onto the surface below.
  • The menu floats to your lower left and follows you. Menu: pin stops it following.
  • Exit AR (or the Meta button) brings you back here to download the report.

Tip: run Settings → Physical Space → Space Setup first so walls, floor and tables show up as wireframe with their sizes.
